# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
All contributors and maintainers are expected to follow it in issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space.

## Additional expectations for this project

* Keep discussion about products factual and verifiable. Vendor representatives are welcome;
  marketing and disparagement of competitors are not.
* Do not share confidential tender documents, pricing or evaluation results from real
  procurements.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
reporting form: open the
[Security tab](https://github.com/jimpe606-maker/apim-requirements/security) and choose
**Report a vulnerability**, and start the title with "Code of conduct". Only maintainers can
read these reports. They are handled confidentially, following the enforcement guidelines of the
Contributor Covenant.
